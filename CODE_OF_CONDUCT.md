# Code of conduct

Hacker CLI Gym welcomes learners and contributors of every experience level.
Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Do
not harass, demean, threaten, or disclose another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive, disruptive, or unsafe content
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct
concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be
reviewed in good faith and handled as confidentially as practical.

By participating, you agree to follow this policy in project spaces and when
publicly representing the project.
